# Lifestyle Retail Sales & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Lifestyle Retail Sales & Business Intelligence Dashboard is an analytics project designed to analyze retail sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, profitability, and regional trends.

The project uses synthetic retail data to demonstrate data cleaning, business analysis, KPI tracking, and interactive dashboard development.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall retail sales and revenue performance.
- Identify profitable products and categories.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Compare sales across different regions and stores.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Python
- CSV and Excel datasets

## Dataset Overview
The project uses synthetic retail data containing:
- 22,000 sales records
- 8,736 customers
- 132 products
- 24 stores
- Sales period: January 2023 – December 2025

## Key Project Components
1. Data Cleaning and Validation
2. Retail Sales Analysis
3. Customer Analysis
4. Product and Category Analysis
5. Regional Performance Analysis
6. Profitability and Discount Analysis
7. KPI Reporting
8. Business Insights and Recommendations

## Business Insights
- Accessories represent a significant share of net revenue in the synthetic dataset.
- Higher discounts are associated with lower profit margins.
- Repeat customers contribute a substantial share of revenue.
- Discount and pricing strategies can influence profitability.
